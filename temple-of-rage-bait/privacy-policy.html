Temple of Rage Bait: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Temple of Rage Bait is a game by Bad GOAT ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no network features. It does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location or usage data.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and any levels you build in the Level Maker are saved on your device only. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example ads or analytics), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
